# EXPERIENCE MAP — The First Minute

The complete emotional timeline of the Claradix landing experience, second by
second, from arrival to the end of the first minute.

This document describes **perception only.** Nothing here is a mechanism.
Timings express intent, not tolerance — a beat may land a breath early or late,
but its *order* and its *feeling* are fixed.

**How to read the entries.** Each second answers seven questions:

- **Sees** — what is objectively in front of the visitor
- **Notices** — where their attention actually is (never the same thing)
- **Moves** — everything in motion
- **Still** — what is deliberately not moving, and holding the frame by it
- **Feels** — the emotion this second must create
- **Hierarchy** — the rank order of attention, first to last
- **Message** — the sentence being written into the visitor below language

Where sound is an event, a **Hears** line appears. Where it is absent, the
silence is the design.

One note on the document's own rhythm: the entries breathe with the scene.
Early seconds are dense because early perception is dense. The final third
grows quiet and short, because the experience does — and because by then, the
scene has handed the minute over to the visitor.

---

## MOVEMENT I — THE DARK · 0:00–0:02

*The doors have just closed. The lights were dimmed for you.*

### 0:00
**Sees:** Black. Total, immediate, everywhere — and within this very first
breath, one thin proof of intent: the faintest line of a horizon, low in the
frame, barely brighter than the dark around it.
**Notices:** The blackness itself — and then the line, because it is the only
thing there is.
**Moves:** Nothing.
**Still:** Everything. The stillness *is* the event.
**Feels:** A single blink of doubt — *is this loading?* — already dissolving,
because the horizon says: no. This dark was made.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the dark · 2. the horizon line.
**Message:** *You have walked into a room where something is about to happen.*

### 0:01
**Sees:** The same black, now with depth — the horizon line settles the frame
into a place: ground below, sky above. The corners of the frame feel darker
than its heart.
**Notices:** That they are looking at a *landscape*, not a screen.
**Moves:** Nothing yet. Perhaps the dark itself seems to breathe once — so
faint it may be imagined.
**Still:** The horizon. The frame.
**Feels:** Orientation. The body settles the way it does in a theatre seat.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the horizon · 2. the dark above it · 3. the dark below it.
**Message:** *This is a place, and it was here before you arrived.*

### 0:02
**Sees:** The first grains: across the low ground, a scattering of
almost-invisible embers fades up out of the black — hundreds of them, resting,
minute, like a city seen from a plane at the moment of waking.
**Notices:** One ember — whichever their eye lands on first. The eye picks a
single light out of a field before it ever sees the field.
**Moves:** Nothing *moves* — the embers arrive by becoming, not by travelling.
**Still:** Every ember holds its place. The horizon holds.
**Feels:** The first touch of wonder. *The dark is inhabited.*
**Hierarchy:** 1. one ember · 2. the field of embers · 3. the horizon.
**Message:** *The darkness was never empty. It was full, and waiting.*

---

## MOVEMENT II — THE WAKING · 0:03–0:08

*The valley remembers it is alive.*

### 0:03
**Sees:** The ember field resolves into terrain — the lights lie on slopes, in
hollows, along a low valley that runs away from the viewer toward the far
softness. A thin mist rests in the lowest ground.
**Notices:** The shape of the land, drawn entirely by where the lights lie.
**Moves:** The faintest breathing begins across the field — embers brightening
and dimming, each on its own slow rhythm, never together.
**Still:** The mountains, now legible as darker-than-dark silhouettes against
the barely-lifted sky.
**Feels:** Depth. The frame acquires kilometres.
**Hears:** The first sound beneath hearing — air, room tone, the world's own
quiet. (If sound is absent, the silence continues seamlessly.)
**Hierarchy:** 1. the lit valley floor · 2. the ridgelines · 3. the mist.
**Message:** *Someone was here before you. Many someones. This is their work,
asleep.*

### 0:04
**Sees:** The breathing spreads. The valley is unmistakably alive now — not
moving, but *awake* in the way a sleeping crowd is awake.
**Notices:** The asynchrony. No two lights breathe together, and somehow that
is what makes it feel real.
**Moves:** Brightness only. Nothing has yet left the ground.
**Still:** Every light's position. The land. The sky.
**Feels:** Patience being rewarded. The visitor who has given four seconds is
being told: keep watching.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the breathing field · 2. the far softness where the valley
dissolves.
**Message:** *Potential. Everything here could move, and is choosing not to
yet.*

### 0:05
**Sees:** The far end of the valley grows very slightly brighter than the
near — the field's breathing leans toward the fog, as if the valley were
listening in that direction.
**Notices:** The direction. Attention is being aimed, and the visitor's eye
goes with it.
**Moves:** A gradient of brightness, migrating far-ward.
**Still:** The near ground quiets slightly — the stage dimming its edges.
**Feels:** Anticipation, first degree. Something is about to happen *over
there.*
**Hierarchy:** 1. the far ground · 2. the fog above it · 3. everything else,
receding.
**Message:** *It begins at the far side. Ideas come from far away.*

### 0:06
**Sees:** The far field is fully awake — embers there burn a shade brighter,
their breathing faster, almost eager.
**Notices:** The contrast between the eager far and the patient near.
**Moves:** Brightness, gathering. Still nothing airborne.
**Still:** The near field, the mountains, the frame.
**Feels:** The gathering before a race. Held energy.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the far field · 2. the near field · 3. the dark between.
**Message:** *Work is about to start. You can feel it the way you feel a room
full of people about to stand.*

### 0:07 — THE SCOUT
**Sees:** One light — a single one, far away — lifts off the ground. It rises a
short way, slows, and hangs in the air above the field it left.
**Notices:** It. Nothing else exists. One moving point in a world of held
stillness is the strongest magnet the eye knows.
**Moves:** The scout, rising, decelerating.
**Still:** Every other light in the world — and this is the whole design of the
second: ten thousand held so one can be seen.
**Feels:** A held breath begins. Curiosity with the chest slightly tight.
**Hears:** The first true sound, if there is sound: one small, clean tone of
lift — the world's first word.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the scout · 2. the ground directly beneath it · 3. nothing.
**Message:** *Every real thing begins as one decision.*

### 0:08 — THE FIRST HELD BREATH
**Sees:** The scout hangs. The valley beneath it has gone almost completely
still — even the breathing has quieted. One light, airborne, alone, against the
fog.
**Notices:** The stillness itself. The visitor may not know they've stopped
scrolling, stopped shifting. They have.
**Moves:** Only the scout's faint hover — a held note, not a motion.
**Still:** Everything. Aggressively, deliberately everything.
**Feels:** The tightening. *What happens now?*
**Hierarchy:** 1. the scout · 2. the silence around it.
**Message:** *Watch.*

---

## MOVEMENT III — THE RIVER · 0:09–0:16

*The valley answers.*

### 0:09 — THE RELEASE
**Sees:** The far field lifts. Hundreds, then thousands of lights leave the
ground together — not as an explosion but as an exhale, each rising along the
slope it rested on, the hillsides seeming to breathe their light upward.
**Notices:** The scale. The single scout has become a nation.
**Moves:** The far half of the valley, airborne. The near field still waits.
**Still:** The mountains — and their stillness is what gives the rising its
size.
**Feels:** Release. The held breath lets go. A quiet gasp-feeling, the one a
crowd makes at fireworks that don't bang.
**Hears:** The air swells — a low rush that grows out of the room tone.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the rising mass · 2. the still near field · 3. the ridges.
**Message:** *When it starts, it really starts.*

### 0:10
**Sees:** The lift spreads toward the viewer like wind crossing a field of
grass — each region of ground releasing in turn, a wave of departure rolling
nearer.
**Notices:** The wave-front. The eye rides it as it comes.
**Moves:** The wave; the risen lights beginning to lean, all in one direction.
**Still:** The ground the wave has not yet reached — including the near
foreground, still asleep at the visitor's feet.
**Feels:** Momentum. The scene has a direction now.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the wave-front · 2. the airborne mass behind it.
**Message:** *This is not an effect. This is a workforce.*

### 0:11
**Sees:** The airborne lights find each other. Individual risings become a
shared drift, the drift becomes a current — a river of light beginning to form
above the valley, flowing across the frame.
**Notices:** The moment of organisation — that instant when many things become
one thing. It is the most watchable transformation there is.
**Moves:** The forming river; stragglers rising to join it.
**Still:** The valley floor, dimmer now, its light gone up.
**Feels:** Awe, arriving. The visitor's face does the small involuntary
softening.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the river · 2. its reflection-glow on the land below.
**Message:** *Many small acts, one intention.*

### 0:12
**Sees:** The river in full flow — the brightest thing the experience has yet
shown, crossing the whole frame, its underside shedding a travelling glow onto
the valley it passes over.
**Notices:** The glow on the land. The world is being *lit by the event* — the
first proof the event is real.
**Moves:** The river; its light moving across the ground beneath it like the
shadow of a cloud, inverted.
**Still:** The mountains, the horizon — the banks of everything.
**Feels:** Awe, held. This is the postcard-second, the one that will be
screenshotted.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the river · 2. the lit ground under it · 3. the dark ahead of
it.
**Message:** *Watch what work looks like when it moves together.*

### 0:13
**Sees:** Inside the river, structure: strands that braid and shear past each
other, faster threads overtaking slower ones, an internal weather.
**Notices:** The interior. The eye, done with the whole, dives into the detail —
and finds the detail is also alive.
**Moves:** Everything within the river; the river within the world.
**Still:** The frame. The world outside the current.
**Feels:** Fascination — the leaning-in feeling.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the braids · 2. the river's edges, where single lights fray
into the dark.
**Message:** *Closeness rewards. There is care at every scale.*

### 0:14
**Sees:** The river reaches across the entire valley now — its head arriving
above the far ground, its tail still fed by the near slopes, the last of the
sleeping field lifting to join.
**Notices:** That the near foreground has finally emptied — the lights that
slept at the visitor's feet are the last ones airborne.
**Moves:** The full length of the river; the final risers.
**Still:** The emptied ground, dark now, waiting to be given something back.
**Feels:** Completeness of motion — everything that can fly is flying.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the river entire · 2. the darkened ground.
**Message:** *All of it is committed. Nothing was held back.*

### 0:15
**Sees:** The river's head begins to slow above the far ground, bunching,
circling — arriving without landing.
**Notices:** The hesitation. After six seconds of confident flow, the river
*waits* — and the change in behaviour snaps attention tight.
**Moves:** The circling head; the still-flowing body.
**Still:** The far ground beneath the circling light — the empty site.
**Feels:** Anticipation, second degree — sharper than 0:05, because now the
visitor knows this world keeps its promises.
**Hears:** The rush thins. The air holds.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the circling mass · 2. the dark ground below it.
**Message:** *It is choosing where to begin.*

### 0:16 — THE SECOND HELD BREATH
**Sees:** The river hangs above the far valley — a slow, bright gyre over dark
ground. One beat. Nothing lands.
**Notices:** The refusal. Everything says *now* — and the world says *not yet.*
**Moves:** Only the gyre, turning.
**Still:** All else. The silence beneath the held rush.
**Feels:** The second tightening — stronger than the first.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the gyre · 2. the waiting ground.
**Message:** *Beginning is a decision, not an accident.*

---

## MOVEMENT IV — THE STANDING · 0:17–0:19

*Something is being built.*

### 0:17
**Sees:** The first descent: lights drop from the gyre to the far ground —
deliberate, vertical, placed. Where they land they stay, and where they stay,
brightness pools: footings. Foundations. The first *fixed* light of the film.
**Notices:** The difference between landing and falling. These lights are
being *set*, like stones.
**Moves:** Falling founders; the gyre feeding them.
**Still:** Each light the instant it lands. Permanence enters the film.
**Feels:** The release into purpose. *It's building something.*
**Hears:** The first settling-sounds — soft, deep, like distant piles driven
into earth, felt in the chest more than heard.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the landing points · 2. the stream feeding them.
**Message:** *Work becomes structure the moment it commits to a place.*

### 0:18 — THE TOWERS
**Sees:** Two verticals begin to rise from the set foundations — one nearer,
one farther, climbing against the sky, each built of light stacking on light.
They are unmistakable within half a second of starting: towers.
**Notices:** The verticality. In a frame that has been horizontal for eighteen
seconds — horizon, valley, river — the first vertical line is a shout.
**Moves:** The two towers, rising; the river feeding their tops.
**Still:** Everything else. The film gives the towers the whole stage.
**Feels:** Recognition beginning — the mind reaching for the name.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the nearer tower · 2. the farther tower · 3. the feeding
stream.
**Message:** *This is construction. Someone is building something real.*

### 0:19
**Sees:** The towers stand complete — two bright masts against the dark sky,
the taller one nearer, both reflected faintly in the mist below. The river,
its work paused, waits in the dark between and above them.
**Notices:** The pause. The towers get one beat of standing alone — long enough
to be *seen* as towers, not just passed through as a stage of a process.
**Moves:** Almost nothing — the towers' light settling, the stream idling.
**Still:** The two verticals, in the exact way lighthouses are still.
**Feels:** Pre-recognition — on the tip of the mind's tongue.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the two towers, together, as a pair · 2. the gap between
them.
**Message:** *Notice the gap. The gap is the point.*

---

## MOVEMENT V — THE DRAPE · 0:20–0:22

*The naming of the thing.*

### 0:20
**Sees:** From the crown of each tower, a stroke of light sets out into the
air toward the other — reaching, unsupported, drawing a rising line across the
gap.
**Notices:** The audacity of it — light walking on nothing.
**Moves:** The two strokes, converging.
**Still:** The towers, the world, the breath.
**Feels:** Suspense in its purest, smallest form: *will they meet?*
**Hierarchy:** 1. the reaching strokes · 2. their launching towers.
**Message:** *Connection is the bravest part of building.*

### 0:21 — THE DRAPE
**Sees:** The strokes meet — and *fall.* The joined line drops into its long
sagging curve, swings once, softly, and settles into the shape every human
alive knows on sight: the draped cable of a suspension bridge.
**Notices:** The curve. There is nothing else in the world for this one
second. This is the single most beautiful frame of the film, and the film
knows it.
**Moves:** Only the settling of the curve — the swing, the stillness taking
hold along it like cold entering water.
**Still:** Absolutely everything else. The world holds the frame for its one
signature.
**Feels:** **The naming.** The click. *It's a bridge.* Recognition arriving as
a physical sensation — a tiny fall of the shoulders, sometimes an audible
breath.
**Hears:** One long, soft tone — the sound of tension arriving in a line and
holding.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the curve · 2. nothing · 3. nothing.
**Message:** *You know what this is. You have always known what this is.*

### 0:22
**Sees:** The second cable drapes beside the first — quicker, a confirmation
rather than a revelation. Beyond each tower, the cables run down and away,
anchoring into the dark ground.
**Notices:** The doubling — the structure becoming rigorous, engineered,
intentional.
**Moves:** The second drape; the anchor-runs.
**Still:** The first cable, already permanent.
**Feels:** Confidence — the visitor now *trusts* the film. It knows what it is
doing.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the paired curves · 2. the towers carrying them.
**Message:** *Once is inspiration. Twice is engineering.*

---

## MOVEMENT VI — THE DECK · 0:23–0:24

*The line comes home.*

### 0:23
**Sees:** Beneath the cables, the deck begins — a clean, level line of light
drawing itself from the far towers *toward the viewer*, fed by the river's
remaining mass. As it advances, fine vertical stitches drop from cable to
deck: hangers, ticking into place behind the moving front like a seam being
sewn.
**Notices:** The direction. It is coming *here.* Toward the visitor's own
ground, toward the bottom of their frame — the first time the film has moved
at them rather than across them.
**Moves:** The advancing deck-front; the stitching hangers; the thinning
river.
**Still:** The completed far structure, receding into the fog it came from.
**Feels:** Inevitability — warm, not threatening. The pleasure of watching a
line be drawn straight at you by a steady hand.
**Hears:** A soft tick beneath the air with each hanger row — a rhythm
emerging, slowing as it nears.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the deck-front · 2. the hangers appearing behind it · 3. the
diminishing river.
**Message:** *This is being built in your direction. It is for you.*

### 0:24
**Sees:** The last of the light arrives: the deck reaches the near ground at
the bottom of the frame, and the final few lights — the last of the river —
settle into their places at the visitor's feet. The bridge is whole. The air
is empty. Nothing is left flying.
**Notices:** The emptiness of the sky — after fifteen seconds of flight, the
air holds nothing, and its emptiness is loud.
**Moves:** The final settlings — small, gentle, done.
**Still:** The bridge. For the first time, the whole bridge.
**Feels:** Completion arriving — but not yet celebrated. A chord resolved but
still ringing.
**Hears:** The air-rush fades to almost nothing.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the whole bridge, seen entire · 2. the near end, at the
visitor's feet.
**Message:** *Done. Every light that rose has landed.*

---

## MOVEMENT VII — THE STILLNESS AND THE CROSSING · 0:25–0:28

*The payoff.*

### 0:25 — THE THIRD HELD BREATH
**Sees:** The finished bridge, alone, complete, still. Two towers, two draped
cables, the stitched deck running from the fog to the viewer's feet. Nothing
moves anywhere in the world. Nothing sounds.
**Notices:** The totality of the stillness — a whole world at rest around one
built thing. This is the frame the film was for.
**Moves:** Nothing.
**Still:** Everything, and this time the stillness is not suspense — it is
reverence.
**Feels:** Quiet weight. Some visitors will feel their own breath pause without
being asked.
**Hears:** True silence — the first since 0:03, and it lands like a held
fermata.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the bridge, whole · 2. the fog it emerges from · 3. the
valley it crosses.
**Message:** *Look at what was made while you watched.*

### 0:26 — THE CROSSING BEGINS
**Sees:** Out of the fog on the far side — the side no one can see into — a
single light emerges onto the bridge and begins to travel the deck toward the
viewer. It is small at first. It is not green. It is the first *warm* light the
world has ever shown.
**Notices:** The warmth. The visitor could not name what changed, but
something changed — the world has been one colour for twenty-five seconds, and
this is not that colour.
**Moves:** The one light, crossing. Nothing else dares.
**Still:** The bridge it walks on; the world around it.
**Feels:** The heart-lift. *Something is coming across.*
**Hears:** A low swell begins — the resonance gathering under the moment.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the traveller · 2. the deck ahead of it · 3. the fog behind
it.
**Message:** *The bridge was never the point. This is the point.*

### 0:27
**Sees:** The traveller grows as it approaches — nearer, larger, warmer,
its glow beginning to touch the deck around it, the hangers it passes
brightening for an instant as it goes by, like a corridor of faces turning.
**Notices:** The bridge acknowledging its traveller — the structure *serving*,
for the first time. Built things exist to be used, and this one is being used.
**Moves:** The traveller; the wake of brightness it draws along the deck.
**Still:** The frame, the towers, the world — an honour guard of stillness.
**Feels:** Rising warmth. The smile-feeling, even in visitors who do not
smile.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the traveller · 2. its wake · 3. the near end of the deck,
waiting.
**Message:** *An idea is arriving. Yours could travel this same road.*

### 0:28 — ARRIVAL
**Sees:** The traveller reaches the near end — the visitor's end — and lands in
a soft, full bloom of warm light at the bottom of the frame. Not an
explosion: a landing. Out of the bloom, as it settles, the first words of the
page begin to exist.
**Notices:** That the light *became the words.* The message did not appear —
it *arrived*, carried across the whole valley, delivered to exactly where
they stand.
**Moves:** The bloom, settling; the words, condensing out of it.
**Still:** The bridge behind, its work done, already dimming with something
like modesty.
**Feels:** **The payoff.** Arrival, warmth, and the small, rare feeling of a
promise kept in front of you.
**Hears:** The one full note of the film — deep, resonant, felt in the
sternum, already fading.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the arrival-bloom and the forming words · 2. the bridge that
delivered them · 3. everything else.
**Message:** *From idea to reality — and it ended up in your hands.*

---

## MOVEMENT VIII — THE WORDS · 0:29–0:34

*The practical world returns, gently.*

### 0:29
**Sees:** The first line of the headline settles into legibility where the
light landed. The scene behind exhales — its brightness stepping back a
half-tone, yielding the stage.
**Notices:** The words — and that they came *from the event*, not from a
template.
**Moves:** The type, finishing its condensation; the scene's gentle dimming.
**Still:** The bridge, the world, the frame.
**Feels:** Attention shifting registers — from watching to reading — without
the temperature dropping.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the headline · 2. the bridge · 3. the valley.
**Message:** *Now we can talk.*

### 0:30
**Sees:** The second line arrives beneath the first — the one carrying the
brand's green, the only green the text will ever need, because here it means
what it has meant all along: the work.
**Notices:** The echo — the words wear the same light the bridge is made of.
**Moves:** Only the arriving line.
**Still:** Everything already placed.
**Feels:** Coherence. The page and the film are one thing.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the completing headline · 2. the bridge beneath/behind it.
**Message:** *The people who wrote this are the people who built that.*

### 0:31
**Sees:** The supporting line settles below — the sentence itself, the one the
whole minute just enacted. Behind everything, the finished bridge's glow now
rests visibly on the valley floor, and the near slopes hold a faint wash of
its light: the world, permanently changed by what was built in it.
**Notices:** Whichever they read first — and peripherally, that the landscape
is no longer the black of 0:00. The event *happened*; the world shows it.
**Moves:** The settling line; nothing else.
**Still:** The bridge, breathing so slightly it is felt rather than seen.
**Feels:** Trust. The quiet kind that precedes a decision.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the sentence · 2. the lit world confirming it.
**Message:** *We did not tell you. We showed you, and then we told you.*

### 0:32
**Sees:** The form arrives — quietly, like furniture placed by someone trying
not to wake the house. A field, a button. No fanfare whatsoever.
**Notices:** Its modesty. After what they just watched, a shouting call-to-action
would be an insult; this one waits.
**Moves:** The form's single soft settling.
**Still:** All the words above it.
**Feels:** Being invited rather than converted.
**Hierarchy:** 1. still the headline · 2. the form · 3. the scene.
**Message:** *If you want to be here when it opens — leave your name. No
pressure lives on this page.*

### 0:33
**Sees:** The remaining furniture takes its places — the small marks, the
links, the line at the foot — each with the same quiet settling, none asking
for attention, all simply *becoming present.*
**Notices:** Almost nothing, which is correct. These are felt as completeness,
not watched as events.
**Moves:** The last settlings.
**Still:** The page, nearly whole.
**Feels:** Order. The room is furnished.
**Hierarchy:** 1. the reading path (headline → sentence → form) · 2. the
scene · 3. the periphery.
**Message:** *Everything is where you expect it. We respect your habits, too.*

### 0:34
**Sees:** The complete page over the living scene: words at rest, bridge
standing, valley lit by it, mist drifting, the far fog holding its soft
secret. The composition, finished, for the first time.
**Notices:** The whole — the one glance that takes it all in and files it as a
single image. This is the frame memory will keep.
**Moves:** Only the world's slow life: mist, breath, the sky's imperceptible
change.
**Still:** All type, forever. The frame, forever.
**Feels:** Settledness. The minute's story is told; the page is now a place.
**Hierarchy:** 1. words · 2. bridge · 3. world — the hierarchy the rest of the
visit will keep.
**Message:** *You were early, and it was worth it.*

---

## MOVEMENT IX — THE LIVING · 0:35–0:60

*The scene is done performing. Now it is simply awake — and it knows you are
there.*

### 0:35
**Sees:** The settled page. The bridge breathes its sub-visible breath.
**Notices:** Their own reading — most are on the sentence or the form now.
**Moves:** Mist, drift, breath. Nothing performs.
**Still:** Structure, type, frame.
**Feels:** Calm.
**Message:** *Take your time.*

### 0:36 — THE INVITATION
**Sees:** Once — only once — the light of the bridge leans, faintly, toward
wherever the visitor's attention rests. A tilt of brightness so slight it
could be imagined. Then it settles back.
**Notices:** Perhaps nothing consciously. Perhaps a flicker of *did it just—?*
That flicker is the entire design.
**Moves:** The one lean.
**Still:** Everything else, hiding the lean by contrast.
**Feels:** The prickle of being noticed. The room is aware of them.
**Message:** *You can touch this.*

### 0:37
**Sees:** The scene, waiting. If the visitor's hand has begun to move toward
it — drawn by the lean — nothing anticipates; the world simply is.
**Notices:** Their own curiosity.
**Moves:** Ambient life only.
**Still:** The bridge, patient.
**Feels:** The small privacy of deciding to try something.
**Message:** *No hurry. It will still be here.*

### 0:38 — THE TOUCH (for those who reach)
**Sees:** Where the hand crosses the bridge, the structure *gives* — light
parting around the touch like deep water, flowing past it, never stopped by
it, closing again behind with unhurried patience.
**Notices:** The generosity of the response — immediate, soft, unmistakable.
And that nothing broke. Nothing ever breaks.
**Moves:** The parting and the slow, mass-like return.
**Still:** The rest of the span, undisturbed — locality is what makes the
touch feel physical.
**Feels:** Delight, the quiet kind. And a touch of tenderness — the thing
*yields.*
**Message:** *Disturb us all you like. We hold our shape.*

### 0:39
**Sees:** The touched region still re-gathering — visibly slower to return
than it was to part.
**Notices:** The asymmetry. Easy to move, effortful to restore: the signature

## Appendix — the attention budget

Most visitors will not give sixty seconds. The map must degrade gracefully by
attention span, and each span must receive something whole:

| Visitor stays… | They must receive |
|---|---|
| 3 seconds | A dark that is unmistakably intentional, holding one living light. Enough to *feel* interrupted rather than bored if they leave. |
| 8 seconds | The waking valley and the scout — one complete, beautiful, closed image: *the dark is full and one light rose.* |
| 15 seconds | The river in full flow — the postcard. They leave with the screenshot-feeling even if they never screenshot. |
| 25 seconds | The naming. They leave knowing they watched a bridge get built out of light. |
| 30 seconds | The crossing and the words. The whole story, delivered. |
| 60 seconds | The touch, the awareness, the place. They leave as someone who was *there*, not someone who watched. |

The single inviolable rule underneath all of it: **whichever second the visitor
leaves on, the scene must be doing exactly one legible, beautiful thing at that
moment.** There is no second of this minute whose honest description is
"in-between."
